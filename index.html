Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our printing services.

What printing services do you offer?

We offer a wide range of printing services including business cards, flex printing, wedding cards, offset printing, banners, posters, stickers, labels, letterheads, and more. If you need something specific, just ask!

How do I place a printing order?

Simply message us on WhatsApp at 0348 5192089 with your requirements. You can share your design file or ask us to create one. We'll send you a quote and a digital proof before printing.

How long does printing take?

Turnaround time depends on the type and quantity of your order. Most small orders (like business cards) are ready within 1-2 days. For bulk orders or custom design work, it may take longer. Contact us for a specific timeline.

Do you offer design services?

Yes! If you don't have a ready design, our team can create custom designs for you. Just share your ideas and requirements, and we'll bring them to life.

Where is Al Fareed Printers located?

We are located at Dabba More Bus Stop, 11½, Orangi Town, Karachi, Pakistan. You can easily find us near the main bus stop.

Do you offer discounts for bulk orders?

Yes, we offer special pricing for bulk orders and regular business clients. Contact us on WhatsApp with your quantity requirements for a custom quote.
